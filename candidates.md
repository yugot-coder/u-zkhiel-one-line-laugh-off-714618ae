## Pick: #1

"My phone's screen-time report this week was just one word: 'Again?'"

Why it wins the judge's criteria, in order:
- Genuinely funny: universal phone-addiction shame, everyone has lived it.
- Hard, sudden landing: the whole joke turns on the last word — a report that
  talks back, exhausted with you. No wind-up wasted.
- Surprise: a screen-time report is a dry statistic; here it becomes a
  disappointed parent delivering a one-word verdict.
- Economy: 74 characters, every word earns its place; nothing left to trim.
- Freshness: not a recycled dad joke or "my AI did X" pun — the twist is the
  report itself judging you, an angle the field's robot jokes won't share.
